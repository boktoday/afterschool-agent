Part 1 — The Idea

An afterschool hour where your teen learns to build, not just consume.

Your teen learns through on-demand training modules they can follow at their own pace, on their own schedule. Each module covers a piece of the build, with step-by-step video and hands-on exercises. Then, every Tuesday at 4:30 PM, we come together for a live Q&A session — bring your questions, get help, and learn alongside other teens. For one hour, we work together to build a personal AI agent using Hermes — a free, local-first agent platform that runs on a laptop or desktop. Set up requires a one-time $5 OpenCode Go account; after that, no data leaves the device for everyday use. The agent's primary job is to guide your teen through the eight essentials of onetoeight — with personalised activities the agent designs for them. Homework help, planning, and research are the bonus, not the point.

Part 2 — The Three Rituals

Three things the agent does every single day.

Through on-demand training modules, your teen configures their agent to perform three daily rituals. By the end, these run automatically — the agent greets them after school, helps them plan, and speaks a one-minute morning briefing when they wake up.

💬
Ritual 1 · After School

The Afterschool Chat

Your teen sits down, opens their agent, and talks. How was the day? What's weighing on you? What's the plan tonight? The agent listens, reflects, and helps them decompress before screen time takes over. It's a grounding conversation — the essential of silence and real community in practice.

Anytime · On-demand
📋
Ritual 2 · Evening

The Planning Session

Before bed, the agent helps them map tomorrow. School tasks, homework deadlines, the essential they're working on this week, one real-world thing they want to do. The agent drafts the plan, your teen edits it, and it saves locally. No app, no account, no cloud sync — just their agent remembering for them.

Anytime · Self-paced
🔊
Ritual 3 · Every Morning

The Morning Minute

This is the magic. Each morning, the agent generates a one-minute text-to-speech audio briefing — their plan, their essential for the week, a thought to carry, a breath to take. Your teen wakes up, presses play, and starts the day with intention instead of a feed. The agent builds it overnight from yesterday's plan.

How does the agent personalise each essential?

The agent isn't reading from a script. During the live sessions, your teen tells the agent about themselves — what they like, what's hard, what their week looks like. The agent uses that to design activities that actually fit. A sporty kid gets a different Body-as-Temple challenge than a bookish one. A teen who's anxious about screens gets a gentler Digital Self-Defence ramp than one who's already cutting back.

Every activity is saved locally with a short reflection. By the end of the programme, your teen has a portfolio of eight personalised essential challenges — and an agent that knows them well enough to keep going.

  • Module 1: Your teen tells the agent their name, interests, and how they're feeling. The agent picks an Essential 1 activity for them.
  • Each module: The agent reviews last time's activity, asks how it went, and designs the next one based on their answer.
  • Every morning: The Morning Minute weaves in the essential they're working on — a reminder, a nudge, a question to carry.
  • By the end: All eight essentials are configured. The agent cycles through them, adapting as your teen grows.

    Before You Enrol — The Tech Requirements

    What you'll need to set this up.

    This is a self-paced build, not a magic install. To get the most out of the Afterschool Agent programme, you'll need these things in place first. None of them are expensive — but they are real.

    💻
    Requirement 1

    Your Own Laptop

    A personal laptop or desktop running Windows, macOS, or Linux — one the teen has full admin access to. Not a school laptop. School-issued machines typically block installations, sandbox new software, and reset on logout — all of which break the agent.

    Minimum spec: 8 GB RAM, 2 GB free SSD storage, modern CPU (any laptop made after 2018).

    🔑
    Requirement 2

    OpenCode Go Account — $5

    A one-time $5 top-up on an OpenCode Go account at opencode.ai/zen/go. This is required to set up the agent system and configure advanced features. The $5 covers the initial agent build and personalisation step — after that, the agent runs primarily on the device itself.

    OpenCode Go gives you access to leading open-source models — DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GLM, Grok, and more — all behind one pay-as-you-go gateway.

    🧠
    Requirement 3 (Optional but Recommended)

    Local AI Model for Ongoing Tasks

    Once set up, the agent runs mostly locally — no ongoing API bills for everyday use. For the best experience, install a small open-source model (we recommend LFM2-700M or similar) directly on the device. It's free, runs offline, and keeps everyday conversations private.

    Keep your $5 OpenCode Go balance topped up as you go for advanced tasks (deep personalisation, weekly curriculum reviews, parent reports). Most families find $5 covers the first few months; ongoing costs depend on how heavy the usage is.

    💬
    Requirement 4

    Telegram or WhatsApp

    The Morning Minute audio briefing and daily check-ins are delivered through Telegram or WhatsApp. Your teen needs a registered account on one of these services on their phone. Both are free and available on any smartphone.

    The agent sends messages, not the other way around — no bots, no spam, no data collection. Just a good morning and a plan for the day.

    The software itself is free forever. The agent lives on your device. The cost is for the gateway access during setup and the occasional advanced query — not for everyday use.

    Part 6 — Questions

    Things parents ask.

    Does my teen need to know how to code?
    No. Hermes is a natural-language agent platform — your teen configures it by talking to it and writing plain-English instructions. We teach everything step by step in the live sessions. If they can use a chat app, they can build an agent.
    What equipment and accounts do we need?
    See the full breakdown in the What You'll Need section above. Short version: a personal laptop (not school-issued) with 8 GB RAM, a one-time $5 OpenCode Go account for setup, optionally a local AI model, and a Telegram or WhatsApp account on a phone for the Morning Minute delivery. The software itself is free.
    Is my teen's data safe?
    Yes. Everything runs on your home computer. No data is sent to the cloud. No accounts to create with third parties. The agent's memory, plans, and morning briefings all live on your device. You can delete everything at any time.
    What if we miss a session?
    No problem. The training modules are on-demand, so your teen can work through them any time. The weekly live Q&A is recorded and made available to enrolled teens. Miss a Tuesday? Catch the recording. The programme is designed so you never fall behind — every module and recording is accessible for as long as you need.
    What age is this for?
    This guide is written for 13-23 year olds. The content is the eight essentials from the onetoeight programme — silence, body, scripture, nature, digital self-defence, life skills, community, and tested character. The agent build is age-appropriate and doesn't require any prior technical knowledge.
    Is this a religious programme?
    No. The programme is fully secular. The eight essentials are practical foundations — silence, body, community, nature, digital self-defence, life skills, knowledge, and tested convictions — that benefit any young person regardless of their background. Your teen tailors the agent to incorporate their own beliefs and values. The agent is theirs to shape. No worldview is pushed, and there's no pressure to think one way or another.
    Can siblings join?
    Yes — one enrolment covers one teen, but siblings can sit in on the webcast and build their own agents on the same computer. Each agent runs independently with its own memory and personality.
    What happens after the programme?
    The agent is yours forever. The software is free. Your teen keeps the three daily rituals running and can extend the agent on their own — for homework, projects, research, anything. The weekly Q&A remains available for as long as you want to keep attending.
