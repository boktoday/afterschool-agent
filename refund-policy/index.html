Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

One-time payment

The Afterschool Agent programme is priced as a one-time payment of $44.88. There are no recurring charges, subscriptions, or hidden fees.

Satisfaction guarantee

If you enrol and decide the programme isn't right for your teen within the first 14 days, you may request a full refund — no questions asked.

How to request a refund

Email boktoday@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request" and include the name and email used during enrolment. We will process your refund within 7 business days.

After 14 days

Refund requests made after 14 days from the date of enrolment are not guaranteed but will be considered on a case-by-case basis. We want you to be satisfied — if something isn't working, reach out and we'll do our best to help.

OpenCode Go fees

The $5 OpenCode Go account top-up is handled entirely by OpenCode and is subject to their refund policy. We cannot process refunds for that fee — please contact OpenCode directly.

No lock-in

You can stop participating in the programme at any time. The Hermes agent software remains free and fully functional on your device regardless of your enrolment status.
