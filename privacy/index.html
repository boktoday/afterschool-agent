Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you enrol in the Afterschool Agent programme or register for early access, we collect your name and email address. This is the only personal information we store.

How we use it

Your email is used to send programme updates, enrolment confirmation, and occasional information about the Afterschool Agent programme. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

What we do NOT do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels on this site. The Afterschool Agent software runs entirely on your own device — no data leaves your computer.

Data storage

Your name and email are stored securely. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us. We will remove your information within 14 days of your request.

Third-party services

The only third-party service we use is Activepieces (via cloud.activepieces.com) to process the early access and enrolment form submissions. Your data is transmitted securely and is not used by Activepieces for any purpose beyond our specified workflow.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, email boktoday@gmail.com.
