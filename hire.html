What I do

Four kinds of work, one rule throughout: the technology serves the people, never the other way round.

01

Custom agents, built for real work

The same craft behind the Afterschool Agent. A personal AI assistant set up on your own machine, taught your routines, your tone and your boundaries. Local-first, so the work stays yours and nothing important is locked away in someone else’s cloud.

02

Workshops and team coaching

Your staff, volunteers or teaching team, from cautious to confident in a day. What AI is good at, where it quietly fails, what should never leave the room, and how to keep a person at the centre of every decision it touches.

03

Family setup evenings

One calm hour at the kitchen table: the agent installed, the parents briefed, the first routines running before bedtime. Built with particular care for neurodivergent families, where the right tool at the right moment can change the shape of a day.

04

Advice and strategy

Choosing tools, setting guardrails, writing the policy, briefing the board. Honest counsel on what to adopt now, what to leave alone for another year, and how to do this without either fear or hype.

How it works

Three steps, and the first one costs nothing but half an hour.

01

A conversation

Thirty minutes, no charge. Tell me what your people need and where AI might fit. If it doesn’t fit, I will say so, and you have lost nothing.

02

A plan

A short, plain plan: what we will build or teach, what it involves, what it costs, and what done looks like. No jargon, no surprises.

03

The work, handed over

I build it, teach it, or coach you through it, and leave you holding something that works without me. Then we are done, or we keep going. Your call.
