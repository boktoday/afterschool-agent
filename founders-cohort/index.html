Your Host — Safe & Vetted

Every live session is run by a screened instructor.

The Afterschool Agent Founders Cohort is hosted by a safeguarding-cleared facilitator. Before any young person joins, the host holds the following verified credentials:

Credential 1

Working with Children Check

State-issued clearance confirming the host is approved to work with minors — the baseline requirement for any programme involving young people.

Credential 2

NDIS Worker Screening

National Disability Insurance Scheme clearance, verifying the host meets the strict conduct and safety standards required to support vulnerable participants.

Credential 3

First Aid & CPR

Current accredited training in emergency first aid and cardiopulmonary resuscitation — because a responsible host is prepared for the physical as well as the digital.

Credential 4

Youth Mental Health First Aid

Accredited training in recognising and responding to mental health challenges in young people — knowing the signs, how to listen, and when to escalate to professional support.
