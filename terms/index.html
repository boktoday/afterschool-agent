Programme description

The Afterschool Agent programme provides on-demand training modules and weekly live Q&A sessions that teach teens how to build and configure a local-first AI agent using the Hermes platform. The programme is priced at a one-time fee of $44.88 per teen.

What's included

  • Access to all on-demand training modules
  • Weekly live Q&A session every Tuesday at 4:30 PM
  • One-on-one help during Q&A sessions
  • Certificate of completion

What's not included

  • Hermes agent software — free and open-source, not part of this payment
  • OpenCode Go account ($5 one-time, required for setup, paid separately)
  • Local AI model hardware or cloud subscriptions

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for providing a suitable laptop or desktop computer, internet access for the live Q&A sessions, and a Telegram or WhatsApp account on a phone for Morning Minute delivery. The teen must have full admin access to their computer.

Programme access

Access to training modules and recorded Q&A sessions is granted upon payment and remains available indefinitely. Live Q&A attendance is not guaranteed if the programme is discontinued, though recorded sessions remain accessible.

Code of conduct

All participants are expected to engage respectfully during live sessions. We reserve the right to remove any participant who disrupts sessions or behaves inappropriately, without refund.
